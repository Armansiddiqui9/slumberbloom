## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a tender, snug, and joyful digital terrarium that turns healthy bedtime routines and outdoor sunlight breaks into delightful botanical companionship. Drawing aesthetic inspiration from contemporary whimsical consumer software, the visual tone blends gentle tactile surfaces, pillowy volumes, and buoyant micro-interactions with an organic, pastoral softness.

The experience strips away all sterile metrics, harsh clinical gamification, and intimidating Web3 jargon. Instead, restful nights manifest as dew-kissed seedlings, offline daytime walks reward users with rare botanical spores, and healthy phone detachment lets digital gardens flourish. The design movement marries **Tactile Softness** with **Warm Organic Minimalism**: squishy, pill-shaped tactile buttons, cream-custard container tiers, softly tinted ambient dropshadows, and cheerful botanical silhouettes that feel like a hand-illustrated nature guide brought to life.

## Colors

The palette radiates pastoral warmth, morning sunlight, and cozy evening comfort. Interfaces rely on whipped buttermilk and clotted cream backdrops rather than cold sterile whites.

- **Primary (`#3E7B54` - Deep Sprout Green):** Serves as the grounded botanical foundation for key active states, primary toggles, brand anchors, and progress indicators.
- **Secondary (`#F7C948` - Buttercup Sun):** Injects warm optimism, morning wake bonuses, glowing plant evolutions, and joyful celebration callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#F4A6B8` - Blossom Blush):** Provides gentle warmth for evening winding-down states, soft milestone hearts, petal accents, and comforting nudges.
- **Neutral (`#20382B` - Forest Ink):** Replaces harsh pure blacks with a deep, organic vegetal charcoal for crisp yet gentle legibility across typography and high-emphasis icons.

### Extended Harmonious Accents
- **Sky Dew (`#8EC5FC`):** A soft water-blue accent dedicated to hydration logs, evening slumber timers, and outdoor air tracking.
- **Mint Foam (`#CDECD9`):** A soothing tint for badge backgrounds, completed progress fills, and passive surface containers.
- **Warm Clotted Cream (`#FAF7EE`):** The foundational global background canvas that prevents eye fatigue in dark bedrooms and soft morning sunlight.
- **Whipped Milk (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated interactive cards, floating pill docks, and tactile button surfaces.

## Typography

The type system blends structured warmth with bouncy, friendly cadence. 

**Epilogue** provides characterful geometric display weight with idiosyncratic curves that evoke mid-century illustrated storybooks and cozy modern editorial interfaces. It is used exclusively for celebratory headlines, specimen names, and growth milestones.

**Plus Jakarta Sans** functions as the workhorse for body copy, micro-labels, metrics, and interactive triggers. Its generous counters, rounded apexes, and open aperture keep small botanical bios and bedtime reminders legible even at dim screen brightness levels. Text is styled in Forest Ink (`#20382B`) rather than pure black to preserve warmth, while secondary descriptions adopt a 70% opacity variant.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize an airy, unhurried cadence with generous vertical breathing room that relaxes the user's nervous system.

- **Layout Structure:** On mobile devices, layouts employ a single-column card stack anchored by a floating bottom capsule navigation dock. On tablet and desktop viewports, content reflows into a 4-to-8 column adaptive card grid with centered container constraints (maximum reading width of 840px for editorial guides; 1120px for garden greenhouses).
- **Rhythm & Touch Targets:** All interactive pressables adhere to a 48px minimum hit target, surrounded by at least `space-sm` separation. Botanical grids preserve an organic rhythm by alternating between full-bleed hero summary cards and twin-column seedling pods.
- **Section Transitions:** Generous section gaps (`space-xl`) keep bedtime progress distinct from social gifting shelves and outdoor expedition logs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold tech gradients or sharp dropshadows, relying instead on **pillowy ambient diffusion** and **organic tonal layering**.

1. **Surface Tier 0 (The Bed):** Warm Clotted Cream (`#FAF7EE`), completely matte, simulating soft uncoated archival paper.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Nestled Pods):** Whipped Milk (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting with an ultra-soft tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(32, 56, 43, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 56, 43, 0.03)`.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Tactile Floating Controls & Action Pills):** Pure white or Buttercup Yellow buttons featuring a dual tactile drop: a micro 2px solid downward push-offset (`0 3px 0 0 rgba(32, 56, 43, 0.12)`) paired with a soft ambient aura (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(32, 56, 43, 0.08)`). When tapped, the element physically depresses downward by 2px with an immediate tactile spring.
4. **Surface Tier 3 (Modals & Bedtime Slumber Overlays):** Deep Forest Ink scrim at 40% blur opacity (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) holding an elevated cloud card cushioned by `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(32, 56, 43, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The shape universe is dominated by ultra-generous organic pills, soft squiggles, and rounded corners that feel like smooth river pebbles or plump succulent leaves. Sharp angles are completely banned to reinforce safety, approachability, and calm.

- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Standard for buttons, search bars, category tags, sleep phase counters, and companion status badges.
- **Garden Cards (`rounded-xl` / 32px):** Used for garden plots, daily sleep summaries, and seedling discovery boxes.
- **Sub-containers & Inset Pods (`rounded-lg` / 20px):** Used for nested statistics, plant diet logs, and outdoor quest modules.
- **Borders:** When outlines appear, they are rendered as soft 1.5px or 2px strokes tinted with 8% opacity Forest Ink (`rgba(32, 56, 43, 0.08)`) to maintain distinct separation without introducing visual tension.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Wake & Water", "Plant Seed"):** Pill-shaped with Deep Sprout Green (`#3E7B54`) fill, Whipped Milk text, and subtle 2px bottom lip shadow. Features a playful 0.97 scale press animation with bouncy spring tension (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`).
- **Secondary ("Snooze Gently", "Inspect Spore"):** Whipped Milk background, 1.5px soft outline, and Forest Ink typography.
- **Celebratory ("Bloom Now"):** Buttercup Sun fill (`#F7C948`) with Forest Ink bold label and an ambient golden halo.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped status indicators with pastel fills (Mint Foam, Blossom Blush, Sky Dew) paired with deep matching text colors.
- Always include an intuitive emoji or micro-botanical vector icon (e.g., a tiny dewdrop, sleeping crescent moon, or sprouting leaf) alongside friendly text such as "Fast Asleep" or "Sun-Soaked".

### Cards
- **Plant Companion Card:** Pure white background, 32px border radius, nested inside a gentle border. Houses an animated botanical avatar centered against an ambient soft pastel backdrop, with health indicators represented as plump water droplets or sunlight beams rather than mechanical progress bars.
- **Sleep Log Card:** Whipped Milk container displaying bedtime duration styled like a cozy sleep report: soft cloud visuals, gentle hours-slept metrics in display typography, and a "dew collected" reward counter.

### Input Fields
- Pill-shaped input tracks filled with Cream Custard (`#F3EEDF`) or Whipped Milk, flanked by a 48px circular icon accessory. Placeholder copy uses friendly, conversational prompts such as "Name your sleepy succulent..." or "Where did you find this seed?". Focused states bloom with a soft 2px Sprout Green halo without harsh outlines.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- **Bedtime Goal Checkbox:** Circular pebble-shaped checkboxes that fill with Sprout Green and blossom into a small checkmark accompanied by micro-confetti or petal bursts upon completion.
- **Detox Toggle Switch:** Pill container shaped like a gentle pod; the thumb is a smooth rounded cream marble with a micro sun or moon glyph debossed in its center.

### Specialized Garden & Sleep Components
- **Seed Discovery Scanner:** A playful viewfinder that replaces complex GPS/AR tech grids with a soft, pulsing organic ring that gently rings out: "Walk near pine trees to discover sleepy spores!"
- **Slumber Cocoon Drawer:** A full-screen sheet that transforms the interface into a warm star-lit forest during bedtime hours, dimming bright hues into muted sage and deep warm midnight greens to preserve natural melatonin levels.
