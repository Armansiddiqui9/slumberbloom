## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Purpose
The design system manifests an entirely new design archetype: **Botanical Cryptoeconomics**. It rejects the cold, hyperactive, predatory aesthetics of speculative Web3 and the sterile, clinical tropes of typical sleep-tracking applications. Instead, it embodies *Proof of Patience*—a tranquil, deeply rewarding environment where restorative slumber and intentional stillness yield living on-chain biodiversity. The interface balances high-craft botanical taxonomy with arcane cryptographic instruments: archival field journals reimagined as living digital artifacts.

### Target Audience & Emotional Resonance
Crafted for rest-deprived digital natives, contemplative crypto collectors, and wellness-focused individuals seeking tactile connection. The interface must evoke:
- **Restorative Serenity:** Calming, moonlit atmospheres that encourage winding down rather than compulsive engagement.
- **Organic Tangibility:** Surfaces that feel pressed, milled, and dew-kissed, conveying grounded earthiness rather than synthetic glass.
- **Quiet Wonder:** The slow, ritualistic pleasure of watching a dormant seed unfurl into a rare botanical specimen while you sleep.

### Visual Style: Botanical Tactile Craft
A hybrid aesthetic blending historical herbarium folios, precision horology, and modern organic tactile interfaces:
- **Parchment & Forest Layering:** Warm cream substrates anchor daytime review, transitioning to celestial nightshade and moss depths during nocturnal mining sessions.
- **Physicality over Speculation:** Skeuomorphic botanical textures, embossed wax stamp seals, hairline golden filaments, and soft debossed card containers establish intrinsic heirloom value.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The color system honors natural diurnal cycles. Unlike harsh neon crypto palettes, the scheme draws directly from old-growth canopies, mineral loam, and nocturnal luminescence.

- **Primary (`#1F3D2B` - Deep Canopy Moss):** The foundational anchor representing dense chlorophyll and ancient vitality. Used for high-emphasis typography, primary interface drivers, and structural frame elements.
- **Secondary (`#4E8752` - Verdant Flora):** Mid-tone living botanical green. Governs active growth progress, confirmed staking state indicators, and healthy botanical telemetry.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Illuminated Amber Dew):** Evokes morning sunlight refracted through droplets of sap and dormant resin. Strictly reserved for harvest moments, reward accrual, proof-of-patience streaks, and rare specimen traits.
- **Neutral (`#F5F1E6` - Milled Cream Parchment):** Tactile, organic paper background tone that minimizes blue-light strain during nocturnal winding down, accompanied by an ultra-bright pure cream substrate (`#FBF9F2`) for elevated surface containers.

### Diurnal Nocturnal Modes
- **Dormancy/Night Mode (`#0E1726` - Celestial Midnight & `#1E293B` - Deep Nightshade):** While the default system initializes in warm milled parchment, engaging the sleep-mining protocol shifts the canvas into a nocturnal twilight palette with deep indigos and muted canopy accents to safeguard circadian rhythms.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Headlines (Newsreader):** Brings an authoritative, archival botanical codex character. Its warm proportions, expressive serifs, and literary grace elevate specimen classifications and ritual state headers above standard tech typography.
- **Body & Technical Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A balanced, humanist sans-serif offering clarity, open counters, and high legibility. It provides modern utilitarian contrast against Newsreader's literary presence.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- Maintain Newsreader exclusively in Regular (400) and Medium (500) weights to preserve editorial delicacy; never apply faux-heavy weights.
- Scientific botanical nomenclature and Latin seed variants must always be rendered in `Newsreader` italic.
- Cryptographic hash identifiers, patience timestamps, and staking epoch ratios use `label-md` or `label-sm` set in medium/semi-bold weights with slight tracking expansion to maintain scanning rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The layout system treats screen viewports as pages in an illuminated botanical field compendium. Content structures are deliberate, spacious, and rhythmic rather than compressed or frantic. Generous negative margins create a sense of stillness and patience.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (Base to 767px):** 4-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and exterior canvas margins. Single-stream specimen tracking and focused card columns.
- **Tablet (768px to 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Allows side-by-side arrangement of active nursery gardens and circadian cycle telemetry.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width container of 1280px, with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) margins. Specimen journals appear alongside living cryptographic growth trees.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals follow a disciplined 4px / 8px baseline rhythm. Interactive cards leverage generous interior padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish breathing room, mimicking museum vitrines and archival mountings.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tactile Botanical Stratification
Depth is conveyed not through generic sterile drop shadows, but through **tonal paper layering, warm ambient under-glows, and debossed organic relief**:

1. **Ground Layer (Canvas Substrate):** Warm pressed cream (`#F5F1E6`) or midnight moss (`#0E1726`) serving as the base field matting.
2. **Surface Layer (Archival Mounts):** Elevated cards in `#FBF9F2` surrounded by ultra-soft botanical ambient tinting: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 61, 43, 0.05)`.
3. **Recessed Wells (Nursery Incubators & Soil Beds):** Inset treatment with a delicate inner shadow (`inset 0px 2px 6px rgba(31, 61, 43, 0.08)`) simulating debossed parchment where sleep hours settle like sediment.
4. **Resonant Glows (Active Patience Mining):** Active resting states employ diffuse radial glows using illuminated amber (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.16)`) and bioluminescent verdant moss (`rgba(78, 135, 82, 0.20)`), creating an organic aura of biological vitality.

## Shapes

### Organic Symmetry
The shape system reflects stone-smoothed geometry and botanical pods. Elements are soft enough to feel tactile and hand-crafted, but structured enough to preserve architectural precision.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `roundedness: 2`):** Applies to micro-components, badges, pill-tags, and interactive action inputs.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Standard container curvature for specimen cards, garden modules, and modal sheets.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Primary greenhouse overview surfaces and seasonal epoch showcases.
- **Wax Seals & Seed Pods:** Circular (`50%` radius) form factors reserved exclusively for specimen seed icons, tactile toggle tokens, and cryptographic patience medals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Sow" / "Harvest" Action):** Background in deep canopy moss (`#1F3D2B`), text in milled cream (`#FBF9F2`). Subtle hairline border in 10% opacity amber. Pressed state reveals an organic micro-dip (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary ("Inspect Specimen"):** Outlined in heartwood moss tint (`rgba(31, 61, 43, 0.2)`) over a neutral cream surface with canopy green text.
- **Dew Accent ("Claim Bloom"):** Amber gradient fill (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`), crisp white/cream typography, emitting a soft amber halo shadow.

### Cards (Botanical Herbarium Folios)
- Framed in `#FBF9F2` with a subtle organic border of `1px solid rgba(45, 90, 62, 0.12)`.
- Cards incorporate top-right Latin taxonomy tags alongside patience duration counters.
- Top section holds an illustrated or generative botanical render resting on an atmospheric circular vignette; lower section displays staking mechanics and sleep lineage stats.

### Chips & Taxon Tags
- Soft rounded pills (`roundedness: 2`) with an earthy moss tint background (`rgba(78, 135, 82, 0.08)`) and deep green text.
- Rarity tiers (Common Sprout, Rare Mycelium, Mythic Nightshade) utilize amber dew borders with accompanying micro-pip dot markers.

### Lists (The Field Log)
- Separated not by generic grey lines, but by warm, low-contrast herbal rules (`1px solid rgba(31, 61, 43, 0.08)`).
- Left-aligned timestamp indicating elapsed rest hours, center botanical discovery label in Newsreader, and right-aligned cryptographic mint hash.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Radios (Patience Seeds)
- Checkboxes mimic seed pod apertures: rounded squares that fill from the center outward with verdant green when active.
- Radio buttons feature a golden dewdot accent (`#F59E0B`) nested in an organic moss circle.

### Input Fields
- Recessed pill-soft inputs with warm cream backgrounds (`#F5F1E6`), subtly contrasting the parent card.
- Inactive border is warm heartwood gray-green; focus ring blooms into a 2px verdant moss ring with an amber focus pip indicator.

### Specialized Component: The Sleep Incubator Dial
- A central ritualistic horological ring tracking nocturnal dormancy.
- As offline hours accumulate, the circular hairline path fills with an illuminated amber vine gradient, blossoming at the threshold of verified restorative sleep epochs.
